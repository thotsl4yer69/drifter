A memory for
your machine.

DRIFTER VIM is a dedicated Raspberry Pi vehicle computer for OBD-II telemetry, drive logging and incident evidence capture.

The idea.

Intermittent faults are difficult to investigate when the symptom clears before a diagnostic session begins. DRIFTER is built to preserve the context around the event: a bounded 90-second pre-event buffer and a 45-second post-event tail.

Captures can be triggered automatically or manually. Ordered material sensor changes support investigation; they are not proof of fault causation.

The build.

Raspberry Pi/Linux provides the local compute. ELM327/legacy OBD and SocketCAN-oriented paths provide vehicle integration. MQTT links telemetry, rules, logging and user interfaces.

The prototype includes a touchscreen field workflow, drive recording and incident capture. Capability depends on the exact vehicle, adapter, protocol and supported PIDs.

The current status.

Hardware-integrated prototype / active hardening. The primary reference vehicle is a 2004 Jaguar X-Type 2.5 L V6. Repeated cold boots, sustained telemetry, adapter recovery and display recovery remain part of the physical acceptance process.

This is not a finished retail appliance or a universal compatibility claim. There are no sales, customer-count or validation-success claims in these materials.
