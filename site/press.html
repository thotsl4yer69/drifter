DRIFTER VIM

Vehicle Intelligence Module — a dedicated Raspberry Pi vehicle computer for OBD-II telemetry, diagnostics, drive logging and incident evidence capture.

LAUNCH STORY The fault vanished. The evidence didn't.

The incident black box can preserve a bounded 90-second pre-event window and 45-second post-event tail around an automatic or manual capture.

CURRENT STATUS

Hardware-integrated prototype / active hardening

Primary physical validation is on a 2004 Jaguar X-Type 2.5L V6. The project targets standards-based OBD-II vehicles, but compatibility is recorded only from real vehicle + adapter evidence.

CLAIM BOUNDARY

Founding beta, not production readiness.

Do not describe DRIFTER VIM as universally compatible, dealer-equivalent or production-ready while the physical acceptance gate remains open.
