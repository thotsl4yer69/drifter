Field edition 01.1 / Site data

Know what
you’re sending.

The illustration.

The time ribbon runs inside your browser. It does not connect to a vehicle, read sensor data or upload your interactions.

The application.

The form prepares text for an email. The website does not submit that text to an application database. Pressing “Prepare email” asks your device to open its email app; you decide whether to send it.

“Copy application” copies the text to your clipboard, or shows selectable text when clipboard access is unavailable. Values remain in the open page only; this site does not save them to local storage.

When you send it.

Applications emailed to MAZLABZ are handled through its business inbox. Use the contact below to ask about your enquiry or request deletion of the email information you provided.

The GitHub vehicle-test form is a separate service. An issue you choose to publish there is public. Do not include private vehicle identifiers or credentials.

The site itself.

No advertising pixels, analytics scripts or application database are included in this version. The hosting provider can process normal request information, such as IP address and requested page, to deliver the website and operate its service.

This page describes this website, not the data handling of the DRIFTER vehicle software or the external email and GitHub services.
