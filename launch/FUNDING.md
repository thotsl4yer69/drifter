# MAZLABZ DRIFTER VIM — Funding Path

**Dated:** 24 September 2026  
**Stage:** hardware-integrated prototype / founding beta

This file is a funding execution map, not a claim of program eligibility. Entity, tax and matched-funding requirements must be satisfied for each program.

## Funding thesis

DRIFTER is strongest when framed as an Australian embedded/automotive R&D and commercialisation project:

- dedicated edge compute in the vehicle;
- OBD-II / legacy transport interoperability;
- deterministic diagnostic and incident-evidence pipeline;
- persistent local data capture;
- human-readable vehicle-link state;
- Raspberry Pi/Linux appliance engineering;
- field validation across heterogeneous vehicle/adapter combinations.

The immediate funding use case is **commercialisation R&D**: hardening the prototype into a reproducible vehicle appliance and validating compatibility, power, enclosure, onboarding and field reliability.

## Priority 1 — CSIRO Kick-Start

**Current status:** open / apply anytime.  
**Funding:** A$20,000–A$60,000 dollar-matched.  
**Use:** a scoped R&D collaboration with CSIRO experts.

Published eligibility includes:
- Australian startup/SME;
- annual turnover and operating expenditure under A$10m, or trading under three years;
- ACN or ICN;
- GST registration;
- capacity to provide matched funding.

### Best DRIFTER project scope

Do not ask CSIRO to build a generic dashboard.

A useful technical research question would be:

> How reliably can a low-cost embedded vehicle node identify and preserve diagnostically useful precursor patterns around intermittent faults across heterogeneous OBD-II vehicles and low-cost adapter transports?

Potential work packages:
- robust cross-vehicle telemetry normalisation;
- evidence quality / missing-data methodology;
- fault precursor feature engineering;
- experimental protocol across multiple vehicles;
- power/reliability/environmental testing;
- edge inference feasibility where it produces measurable diagnostic value.

## Priority 4 — Australian R&D Tax Incentive

**Current status:** open under current rules until announced changes begin 1 July 2028.

Published baseline:
- eligible R&D entity must be a company meeting the program definition;
- activities assessed individually, not merely because the project is called R&D;
- core activity outcome must not be knowable in advance and must follow a systematic progression of work;
- minimum eligible R&D expenditure generally A$20,000, subject to stated exceptions;
- companies under A$20m aggregated turnover may access a refundable offset based on corporate tax rate + 18.5% premium under current rules.

### DRIFTER recordkeeping to start now

For each technical experiment capture:
- hypothesis / technical uncertainty;
- why a competent professional could not determine the outcome in advance;
- experiment design;
- hardware + software version;
- observations;
- failed attempts;
- analysis;
- conclusion / next experiment;
- labour/material/service costs linked to the activity.

Do not retrospectively invent R&D records.

Candidate experimental streams:
1. robust OBD transport negotiation/recovery across heterogeneous adapters;
2. pre/post fault evidence-window effectiveness;
3. cross-vehicle normalisation and PID availability;
4. power-loss recovery and storage integrity;
5. display/vehicle-link recovery behaviour;
6. edge diagnostic inference under Pi resource constraints.

Obtain professional tax/R&DTI advice before claiming expenditure.
